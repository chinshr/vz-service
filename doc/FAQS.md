## What media files do you support?

We support almost all media file types (audio and video), including aac, aif, m4a, mov, mp3, mp4, wav, wma, and many more.

## Is there any media size or length limitation?

It depends. Currently, our Free plan allows only a limited media length. Paid plans provide a variety of recording length. See the different [options](/pricing) for more details.

## How accurate is the transcription?

The better quality the audio the better the transcription will be. Things that impact the accuracy of the transcription are:

* Clarity of the speech
* Background noise
* Cross talk or multiple speakers at the same time

We use the best speech technology available today, but know that we cannot be the best at every use case. Best to give us a try and see if our system works for your use case.

## Do we support speaker segmentation?

Yes, VOYZ.ES provides speaker segmentation.
